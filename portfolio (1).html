README.md — Project 01 · Excel · Retail Analytics
📊 Retail Sales Dashboard — Uncovering a ₹2Cr Revenue Leak
ExcelPower QueryDashboardRetail
🎯 Executive Summary

Analyzed 3 years of retail data (150K rows). Found one product subcategory drove 34% of all returns — a ₹18L/quarter cost invisible in raw data. Dashboard enables weekly regional reviews in under 5 minutes.

💼 Business Problem

Regional manager needed to understand why Q3 missed targets by 12–18% despite steady traffic. Existing reports showed totals — not drivers.

▸ Q3 dip = seasonal demand mismatch, not low traffic

Phase 5: build ML return-rate prediction model. Integrate live Power BI refresh for automated weekly reporting.

README.md — Project 02 · SQL · Finance
🏦 Banking Churn Deep-Dive — SQL Investigation Saving ₹5Cr Annually
PostgreSQLCTEsWindow FunctionsFinance
🎯 Executive Summary

Advanced SQL analysis on 10K banking customers reveals customers with fewer than 3 products churn at 4× the average rate — a targeted retention play worth ₹5Cr/year.

💼 Business Problem

Retention team had churn rates but no idea which customer segment to prioritize. Random campaigns wasted budget with low ROI.

🔧 Methodology
  • Cohort analysis using CTEs and RANK() window functions
  • Used LAG() to find churn inflection points by customer tenure
  • 15+ documented queries with business context in comments
📈 Results

▸ Less than 3 products = 4× average churn rate
▸ Month 6–9 is the highest-risk churn window
▸ Recommend: cross-sell campaigns at Month 4–5 with bundle offers

🔭 Next Steps

Phase 5: train a churn prediction model using this SQL-derived feature set. Deploy as a weekly risk score report for the retention team.

README.md — Project 03 · Environmental Analytics
Urban Stress Intelligence — Global Environmental Anomaly Analysis
PythonSQLPower BIEnvironmental Analytics
Executive Summary

Analyzes daily environmental observations across Delhi, Tokyo, and New York to identify unusual conditions, contributing signals, and persistence. The dataset contains 1,098 city-day records for 2024.

Research Question

Where are environmental conditions unusually abnormal, what signals contribute to those anomalies, and how persistent are the patterns?

Methodology
  • Prepared and analyzed environmental data using Python, Pandas, SQL, and API-based workflows
  • Applied standardized anomaly signals and a compound scoring approach
  • Built five Power BI modules for overview, city comparison, anomaly detection, driver analysis, and data quality
Key Findings

▸ 129 anomaly days were detected across the three cities under the current method
▸ PM2.5 was the most frequently recorded driver (51 driver-days), followed by wind (47), heat (20), and fire detections (14)
▸ Driver counts can overlap on the same day; results depend on baseline and thresholds

Limitations and Next Steps

Missing observations and uneven sensor coverage affect comparisons. Satellite fire detections indicate thermal anomalies, not confirmed fires. The analysis is exploratory and does not establish causation or health impacts. Next steps include documenting data sources, adding reproducibility instructions, and publishing dashboard screenshots with the project code.

README.md — Project 05 · Machine Learning · HR Analytics
🧠 Employee Attrition Predictor — Flagging At-Risk Talent Before They Resign
PythonScikit-learnRandom ForestHR Analytics
🎯 Executive Summary

Trained a Random Forest classifier on IBM HR data (1,470 employees), achieving ROC-AUC of 0.789. Identifies monthly income, age, and overtime as the strongest attrition drivers, with individual risk scoring for proactive HR intervention.

💼 Business Problem

16.1% annual attrition (237 exits/year) with no way to flag at-risk employees before they resign. Each departure costs 50–100% of annual salary to replace.

🔧 Methodology
  • EDA across 1,470 employees surfaced overtime, income, and tenure patterns before any modeling
  • Diagnosed class imbalance — untuned model defaulted to predicting "stayed" for nearly everyone
  • Applied class_weight={0:1, 1:4} — lifted recall on actual leavers from 0.11 to 0.30
  • Converted output into three risk tiers (High / Medium / Low) for HR prioritization
📈 Results

▸ ROC-AUC: 0.789 · Recall: 0.30 · Precision: 0.48 on the attrition class
▸ Monthly income and age ranked above overtime as top predictors
▸ Modeled ₹29.5L annual saving if attrition drops 4 percentage points

🔭 Next Steps

Apply SMOTE for further imbalance correction, add SHAP for individual-level explanations, and wrap in a Streamlit app for HR self-service queries.

README.md — Project 06 · Nexus AI Business Intelligence
🤖 Nexus AI Business Intelligence Assistant — Evidence-Grounded Business Reports
PythonStreamlitChromaDBGemini API · RAG
🎯 Executive Summary

Nexus AI is a Streamlit business intelligence assistant that lets users upload PDF or TXT business reports and ask questions in natural language. Its retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow retrieves relevant report content to support answers with source evidence.

💼 Business Problem

Business reports can be long and difficult to search quickly. Analysts and decision-makers need a faster way to locate supporting evidence and extract useful information without treating an unsupported model response as a fact.

🔧 Methodology
  • Upload PDF/TXT business reports and extract text for retrieval
  • Use ChromaDB to support retrieval of relevant content within the RAG workflow
  • Use the Gemini API to generate natural-language responses grounded in retrieved report content
  • Keep deterministic business rules available for supported revenue-ranking tasks
🧰 Core Features

▸ Report question answering with source evidence
▸ Analytics Dashboard for report-level exploration
▸ Report Data Extractor for turning report content into structured data
▸ Streamlit interface designed for practical business analysis

✅ Responsible Use

Answers depend on the content and quality of uploaded reports. Review the cited evidence before making a business decision; retrieval and generative answers can still miss context or contain mistakes.

🔭 Next Steps

Continue testing across different report formats and business questions, improve retrieval quality, document deployment steps, and keep API credentials in Streamlit Secrets rather than committing them to the repository.
